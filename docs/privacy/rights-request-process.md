# Data-subject rights and complaints — internal process

Internal document, 2026-07-31. Owner: **[DECIDE — name the responsible
person]**. There is no DPO and none is invented; requests are handled by the
business owner.

## Current reality

Until the enquiry transport and mailbox exist, the website stores no personal
data, so there is nothing a rights request could be executed against on the
website side. This process exists so it is ready the day that changes.

## Channels

- Email to the privacy contact (hello@optaradigital.com — **blocked: no
  mailbox exists yet**; the same address the Privacy Policy lists).
- Any request arriving through the contact form or correspondence is treated
  identically — a rights request does not need magic words.

## Handling steps

1. Acknowledge receipt promptly; record the date (statutory clock: one
   calendar month, extendable for complex requests with an explanation).
2. Verify identity proportionately — normally by replying to the email address
   we already hold for that person; do not demand ID documents for trivial
   requests.
3. Search locations (once they exist): the enquiry inbox, email
   correspondence, the email delivery provider's stored messages, any future
   CRM. Today: none.
4. Coordinate with processors where data sits with them (Vercel logs cannot be
   selectively purged by us — say so honestly and explain platform retention).
5. Respond in plain language: what was found, what was done, any limits and
   why (e.g. accounting records that must be kept).
6. Keep a simple log of requests and outcomes.

## Rights that can apply

Access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection (to the
legitimate-interests processing), portability (unlikely to be engaged — no
automated contract-based processing at scale), withdrawal of consent (nothing
currently relies on consent).

## Complaints

1. Complaints about data handling go to the same contact; acknowledge, review
   by the owner, respond with reasons.
2. Keep records of complaint, review and response.
3. Every response includes: the person may complain to the Information
   Commissioner's Office — ico.org.uk, or 0303 123 1113.
4. DUAA 2025 note: complaint-handling expectations for controllers have been
   strengthened; keep this process documented and check current ICO guidance
   at legal review. **Flagged for the reviewer.**

## Unexpected children's data

The site is aimed at businesses and not directed at children. If an enquiry
plainly comes from a child: do not use it commercially, delete it once any
safeguarding consideration is ruled out, and record the decision.
